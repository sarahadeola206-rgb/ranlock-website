Questions

Visualizer FAQ

Is the roof visualizer free to use?
Yes — 100% free, no account required. The Owens Corning Design EyeQ Visualizer is provided as a free planning tool for homeowners considering a new roof. Use it as many times as you like.
Can I upload a photo of my own home?
Yes. The visualizer supports uploading your own home photo so you can see exactly how different shingles look on your actual roof — not just a stock image. Best results come from a straight-on photo of your home's front elevation.
How accurate is the visualizer?
The visualizer gives you a very good approximation of shingle colors and styles. That said, screen calibration and lighting conditions vary. We always recommend viewing physical shingle samples in natural light at your home before finalizing your selection — and Ranlock can bring samples to you at no charge.
What if I find a shingle color I love — what's next?
Note the shingle name and collection (e.g., "Duration® Driftwood") and include it when you fill out our estimate request form. Our team will confirm availability, show you samples in person, and include it in your project quote at no extra charge for the consultation.
Does Ranlock install all Owens Corning shingle lines?
We primarily work with the Owens Corning Duration®, TruDefinition® Duration, and Berkshire® collections — the lines best suited to Houston's climate. We'll recommend the right product for your home, budget, and performance goals during your free inspection.
