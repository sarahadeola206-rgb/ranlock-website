# RANLOCK AI Marketing Operating System

This repository is designed to function as a permanent AI marketing department for RANLOCK Roofing & Construction. An Executive Marketing Director coordinates specialized AI agents across every marketing channel, with performance measured against appointments, revenue, and profit—not lead volume alone.

## Contents

The operating system covers:

- Executive launch instructions
- Master AI agent instructions
- Meta Ads
- Social media
- Video
- CRM lifecycle
- Analytics and KPI tracking
- SEO and Google Business Profile
- Website conversion
- Storm-response playbooks
- Creative standards
- Competitive intelligence
- Weekly reporting templates
- Shared branding, authority controls, and operating procedures

## Purpose

The system is intended to:

1. Audit every marketing asset.
2. Connect Meta, Google, CRM, website, analytics, and social platforms.
3. Build a 90-day performance baseline.
4. Launch coordinated campaigns.
5. Produce weekly executive reports.
6. Continuously optimize based on appointments, revenue, and profit.

## Assessment

The current concept provides a strong foundation for an integrated marketing operation. Its next stage is to connect strategy, execution, measurement, and sales outcomes in one governed system.
