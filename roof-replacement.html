Know When It's Time

6 Signs Your Houston Roof
Needs Replacement

Houston's climate is brutal on roofs. If you're seeing any of these, it's time for a free Ranlock inspection.

🔍Age Over 15–20 Years

Most asphalt shingle roofs in Houston's climate last 15–25 years. If your roof is approaching or past this range, a proactive replacement is smarter than waiting for a failure.

🌿Granules in Gutters

Excessive granule loss in downspouts or at the base of your downspouts means shingles are deteriorating. Bare spots in the shingle surface leave the asphalt directly exposed to UV.

🌊Curling or Cupping Shingles

Shingles that curl upward at edges (curling) or cup in the center have lost their structural integrity. They lift in wind and no longer seal properly against rain.

💧Interior Water Stains

Ceiling stains or attic moisture are a sign the roof system has been breached. Often indicates underlayment failure, flashing issues, or shingle damage that has allowed water penetration.

🌑Dark Streaks or Algae

Black or dark streaking is blue-green algae (Gloeocapsa magma) — extremely common in Houston's humidity. While treatable, repeated algae growth signals shingles without StreakGuard™ protection.

⚡Recent Storm Damage

After hail or high winds, damage is often not visible from the ground. Microscopic hail impacts fracture the shingle mat and accelerate premature failure — only a professional inspection reveals the full picture.

Common Questions

Roof Replacement FAQ

How long does a roof replacement take in Houston?
Most residential roof replacements in Greater Houston are completed in one full day — our crew arrives early, tears off the old system, installs the new one, and cleans up. Larger or more complex roofs may require two days. We'll give you an accurate schedule during your free inspection.
How much does a roof replacement cost in Houston?
Most Houston roof replacements range from $8,000 to $22,000+ depending on home size, roof pitch and complexity, shingle selection, and deck condition. The best way to know your exact cost is a free Ranlock inspection — we measure accurately and provide an itemized written quote.
Will my insurance cover roof replacement?
If your roof was damaged by a covered event — hail, wind, or hurricane — your homeowner's insurance will typically cover the full replacement minus your deductible. Ranlock inspects for storm damage, documents everything thoroughly, and meets your adjuster on-site to help you get paid correctly.
What Owens Corning shingles does Ranlock install?
Ranlock installs the full Owens Corning shingle lineup — Duration® (most popular, 130 mph wind rated), TruDefinition® Duration (enhanced color), and Berkshire® Collection (premium slate-style). We also install the complete system components: leak barrier, underlayment, starter strips, and hip & ridge shingles. Ask about impact-resistant options for hail-prone areas.
Do you handle the permit?
Yes — Ranlock handles permitting requirements for your jurisdiction. Requirements vary across Houston, Harris County, Galveston County, and Fort Bend County. We know the local requirements and include permit coordination in your project.
