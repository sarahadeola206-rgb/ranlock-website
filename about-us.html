Why We Started

Houston Deserves
Better Roofing

The Greater Houston area has seen an explosion of roofing contractors — many of them storm chasers who appear after major weather events, collect insurance payments, and disappear before the job is properly done. Homeowners are left with unfinished work, failed inspections, and no one to call when problems emerge months later.

Ranlock was founded to be different. We're a local company — rooted in the Houston area, building relationships with homeowners in Webster, League City, Pearland, Pasadena, and across Greater Houston. When you call us, you get a real person. When we commit to a project, we see it through.

We partner exclusively with Owens Corning because they make products we're proud to install — and because their warranty system protects our customers long after we've finished the job. Better products, better pricing, better warranties. That's not just a tagline — it's the standard we hold every project to.
