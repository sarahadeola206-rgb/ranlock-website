What Affects Your Price

Houston Roof Replacement Cost Factors

Every roof is different. Here are the key variables that determine your exact replacement cost in Greater Houston.

Roof Square Footage

The most significant cost driver. We measure actual roof surface area (squares), which accounts for pitch and slope — not just floor area.

Roof Pitch & Complexity

Steep pitches and complex roof lines (multiple valleys, hips, dormers) require more time, safety equipment, and labor — increasing cost per square.

Shingle Selection

Standard architectural shingles, premium collections, impact-rated, or metal roofing — your material choice significantly affects both cost and longevity.

Decking Condition

Rotted or damaged decking boards discovered during tear-off add to cost. We assess and disclose any deck issues before work begins — no hidden charges.

Full System Components

A proper roof system includes underlayment, ice & water shield, starter shingles, hip & ridge, and ventilation — all of which factor into the total cost.

Insurance Coverage

Storm damage claims can dramatically reduce or eliminate your out-of-pocket cost. Ranlock helps you file accurately to maximize your coverage payout.

Common Questions

Roof Cost FAQ

How much does a roof replacement cost in Houston?
In Greater Houston, most residential roof replacements range from $8,000 to $22,000+ depending on home size, roof complexity, shingle selection, and deck condition. Smaller homes with simple roofs and standard shingles fall on the lower end; larger or more complex homes with premium materials fall higher. The best way to know your exact cost is a free Ranlock inspection.
Will my insurance cover roof replacement?
If your roof was damaged by a covered event like hail, wind, or a hurricane, your homeowner's insurance will likely cover most or all of the replacement cost (minus your deductible). Ranlock inspects for storm damage, documents it thoroughly, and helps you navigate the claims process to ensure you're not underpaid.
How accurate is this online estimator?
The estimator gives you a rough ballpark range based on typical Houston-area pricing. Your actual cost could be higher or lower depending on your exact roof measurements, current material costs, labor scheduling, deck condition, and other factors discovered during inspection. Use it to budget — not to compare quotes.
Is your inspection and estimate really free?
100% free, no obligation whatsoever. We inspect your roof, document everything, and provide you a written itemized estimate at no charge. You're under no pressure to hire us. We earn your trust by being thorough and honest — the estimate is part of that process.
How long does a roof replacement take?
Most Greater Houston residential roof replacements are completed in 1 full day. Larger or more complex roofs may take 2 days. We'll give you an accurate schedule during your inspection so you can plan accordingly.
