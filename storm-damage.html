Types of Storm Damage We Handle

We Repair Every Type of Storm Damage

Greater Houston faces every type of severe weather — and Ranlock has the experience to handle the damage each one leaves behind.

🌧 Hail Damage

Hail impacts cause granule loss, dented metal, cracked shingles, and compromised waterproofing — often invisible from the ground. Our detailed inspection documents every impact point for your insurance claim.

💨 Wind Damage

High winds lift, crack, and tear shingles — especially at edges, ridges, and hip lines. Even 60 mph gusts can create vulnerable spots that lead to leaks in the next rain.

🌀 Hurricane Damage

Hurricanes and tropical storms create sustained damaging winds, flying debris, and heavy rain penetration. Ranlock responds quickly after major storm events to assess, document, and repair.

🌳 Fallen Tree / Debris

Tree branches and debris driven by storm winds cause direct structural damage — broken rafters, punctured decking, crushed shingles — that require immediate attention to prevent interior damage.

🌊 Water Intrusion

Storm-compromised roofs allow water to infiltrate the attic, insulation, and ceilings. We identify the source, stop the leak, and repair the full damage path — not just the symptom.

🏠 Flashing Failure

Wind and debris commonly damage or dislodge roof flashing around chimneys, vents, and skylights — often the first point of post-storm water intrusion even when shingles appear intact.

Common Questions

Storm Damage FAQ

How do I know if my roof has storm damage?
Many types of storm damage — especially hail granule loss and subtle wind lift — aren't visible from the ground. Signs you might notice include missing shingles, dented gutters, granules collecting in downspouts, or water stains on ceilings. The safest approach after any significant storm is to schedule a professional inspection. Ranlock provides free storm inspections with no obligation.
Will my insurance cover the full roof replacement?
Most standard homeowner's policies in Texas cover roof replacement when damage is caused by a covered peril — hail, wind, and hurricane are typically covered. Your out-of-pocket cost is usually your deductible. The key is proper documentation — Ranlock photographs and measures all damage and meets your adjuster to ensure nothing is underpaid or missed.
How quickly can you respond after a storm?
We prioritize storm damage calls and aim for same-day or next-day inspections in most cases. During major storm events affecting large parts of Greater Houston, we schedule in the order calls are received. Call 281-299-1086 immediately after a storm to get in the queue.
Can I file a claim myself without a contractor?
You can — but having an experienced contractor present during the adjuster's inspection dramatically increases the chance that all damage is properly documented and your claim reflects the true scope of work needed. Many homeowners who attempt this alone receive underpaid settlements. Ranlock provides this support at no charge as part of our service.
