Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before calling — answered honestly.

Are you licensed and insured in Texas?
Yes. Ranlock Roofing & Construction LLC is fully licensed and insured in Texas. We carry general liability insurance and workers' compensation coverage on every job. You'll never be left exposed.
Do you offer free inspections and estimates?
Absolutely. Every inspection and every estimate is 100% free with no obligation. We'll inspect your roof, document any damage, and give you a clear written quote — no pressure, no surprises.
What shingle brands do you use?
We primarily install Owens Corning shingle systems — including the Duration, TruDefinition Duration, and Berkshire collections. Owens Corning is one of the most trusted names in roofing, and their products come with strong manufacturer warranties.
Can you help with my insurance claim?
Yes. We have extensive experience helping homeowners navigate the insurance claims process. We can document damage, meet with your adjuster, and ensure your claim accurately reflects the scope of repairs needed.
How long does a roof replacement take?
Most residential roof replacements in the Greater Houston area are completed in 1–2 days. Larger homes, complex roof designs, or weather delays can extend that timeline. We'll give you a realistic schedule before work begins.
Do you handle more than just roofing?
Yes — Ranlock is your full exterior and interior remodeling partner. We handle siding, gutters, windows, patio covers, decks, fencing, interior remodeling, and more. One trusted contractor for your whole home.
What areas do you serve?
We serve all of Greater Houston including Houston, Webster, League City, Baytown, Sugar Land, Pasadena, Pearland, Clear Lake, Cypress, Spring, Tomball, The Woodlands, Texas City, Friendswood, Deer Park, and surrounding communities.
What warranties are available?
Owens Corning products carry strong manufacturer warranties on materials. Additionally, Ranlock backs our workmanship. Exact warranty terms depend on the product and system installed — ask us for details on your project.
