Why Every Layer Matters

The Complete
Roofing System

Most homeowners only think about shingles — the visible top layer. But a proper roof is a complete system of six components, each performing a specific job. Missing even one layer compromises the entire system's performance and your warranty coverage.

Ranlock installs the full Owens Corning Total Protection Roofing System — every component, every time. It's why our roofs perform better and last longer in Houston's demanding climate.

6
Hip & Ridge Shingles
Caps the peak and hip lines — completes the look and seals the highest-exposure points of your roof.
5
Shingles (Duration® / TruDefinition®)
The primary weather barrier — engineered for 130 mph wind resistance, UV protection, and algae resistance in Houston's climate.
4
Starter Strip Shingles
Installed at eaves and rakes — seals edges against wind-driven rain. An often-skipped component that directly affects wind performance.
3
Deck Protection (Underlayment)
Synthetic underlayment installed directly over the decking — secondary moisture barrier and critical for warranty validity.
2
Leak Barrier (Ice & Water Shield)
Self-sealing membrane at eaves, valleys, chimneys, and penetrations — the last line of defense against water intrusion at vulnerable points.
1
Roof Deck (Decking)
The structural foundation — plywood or OSB sheathing that everything attaches to. Damaged decking is replaced during a full roof installation.

Plus ventilation — proper attic airflow extends shingle life and maintains warranty coverage.

Questions

Roofing System FAQ

Does it matter which components I choose for my roofing system?
Yes — significantly. The system's weak point determines its performance level. Using a premium shingle with a cheap underlayment and no starter strip undermines the whole investment. More importantly, some enhanced warranty coverage levels require using matching Owens Corning system components throughout. Ranlock helps you select the right combination for your budget and goals.
What is the Owens Corning Total Protection Roofing System?
It's Owens Corning's complete six-layer roofing system: leak barrier, deck protection (underlayment), starter strip shingles, shingles, hip & ridge shingles, and attic ventilation. When all components are installed together as a system, they work synergistically for maximum performance — and may qualify for stronger warranty coverage than any single product alone.
What's the difference between a roof repair and a full roof system replacement?
A repair addresses a specific problem — a few damaged shingles, a flashing failure, a localized leak. A full system replacement is a complete tear-off and reinstallation of every component from the deck up. If your roof is over 15–20 years old or has widespread damage, a full replacement typically offers better value than ongoing repairs. Ranlock's free inspection will give you an honest recommendation based on your actual roof condition.
How important is attic ventilation for my roof?
Critical — especially in Houston. Inadequate attic ventilation traps heat (attic temps can exceed 160°F in summer), which bakes shingles from below, accelerates aging, and can void your manufacturer warranty. It also drives up cooling costs. Ranlock assesses your current ventilation during every inspection and includes it in system recommendations.
