Owens Corning Technology

Why Owens Corning Shingles Outperform

Every Owens Corning shingle Ranlock installs is backed by decades of engineering — built to handle the exact conditions Houston homeowners face every year.

SureNail® Technology

An exclusive nailing zone with a reinforced fabric strip gives nails a dramatically stronger hold. In Houston wind conditions, this means shingles stay put through 130 mph gusts — up to 2× the holding power of standard shingles.

TruDefinition® Color

More contrast, richer tones, and multi-dimensional color blends. TruDefinition technology makes shingles look more like natural slate or wood — and the colors stay vibrant longer under Houston's relentless UV exposure.

StreakGuard™ Algae Resistance

Houston's heat and humidity create perfect conditions for algae growth — causing those dark streaks you see on older roofs. StreakGuard granules prevent algae colonization and keep your roof looking clean for years.

Strong Manufacturer Warranties

Owens Corning products come backed by solid manufacturer warranties on materials. Ask Ranlock about specific warranty coverage available for your project and product selection — we'll explain it all before you sign.

Impact Resistance Options

Class 4 impact-resistant shingles are available for hail-prone Houston neighborhoods. These may also qualify you for insurance discounts — ask your Ranlock inspector whether impact-rated shingles make sense for your property.

Energy Star® Options

Select Owens Corning shingles carry Energy Star ratings with solar-reflective granules that reduce attic heat transfer. In Houston summers, this can meaningfully lower cooling costs over the life of the roof.

Questions Answered

Shingle Roofing FAQ

What is the best shingle for Houston's climate?
For most Houston homes, Owens Corning Duration® or TruDefinition® Duration shingles are the top choice. They feature SureNail® Technology for high-wind resistance (130 mph rated), StreakGuard™ algae protection for Houston's humidity, and strong manufacturer warranties. For a premium look, the Berkshire® Collection delivers slate-like aesthetics with the same performance engineering.
How long do shingles last in Houston?
In Houston's climate, quality architectural shingles typically last 20–30 years. However, extreme heat, intense UV, and severe weather events can shorten that timeline. Proper installation, adequate attic ventilation, and quality underlayment all significantly affect shingle lifespan — which is why the full roofing system matters, not just the shingle.
Can I choose my shingle color before committing?
Absolutely. Use the Owens Corning Roof Visualizer on this site to explore colors on a home like yours. We can also bring physical shingle samples to your home so you can see them in natural light next to your actual exterior colors. We never rush the selection process.
Are impact-resistant shingles worth it in Houston?
Often yes — especially in areas that see frequent hail. Class 4 impact-resistant shingles can withstand larger hail impacts with less damage, potentially reducing insurance claims. Many Houston-area insurers also offer premium discounts for impact-rated roofs. Ask your Ranlock inspector whether the cost difference makes sense for your neighborhood and insurance situation.
What's the difference between 3-tab and architectural shingles?
3-tab shingles are flat, single-layer shingles with a uniform appearance. Architectural (laminate) shingles like the Owens Corning Duration series are multi-layer, heavier, and create dimensional depth and shadow lines. Architectural shingles last longer, look better, offer superior wind resistance, and carry better warranties — they're the standard for virtually all Houston roof replacements today.
